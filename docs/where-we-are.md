## How the last ones went

Nothing here is needed to read the finished code; it is here because the
method that finished the project is not the method that started it.

The parks' headers carried a word count, a DIFF and `advise.py`'s aligned
findings, generated by `tools/decomp/parkstats.py`. `advise.py`'s verdict said
what class a park was in: `ALLOC` (every opcode, immediate and displacement
already matching; only register numbers differ), `SHAPE` (the C is wrong) or
`LENGTH` (word counts differ). `docs/landing-a-function.md` was the entry
point for an agent given a function name, and for `ALLOC` and shape search the
first move was the permuter (`docs/permuter-protocol.md`) rather than sweeping
by hand -- search costs CPU, reasoning costs context, and the measured marginal
cost of a landing rose 17x between 2026-10-23 and 2026-10-29 (3.6M raw tokens
per function to 61M).

**That stopped being true for the last thirteen.** Each had had the permuter
and `search.py` run on it without reaching a match -- func_8005B4D0 alone left
3753 permuter output directories behind -- so from 2026-10-30 they were worked
by hand from the dumps and the gcc source, with `verify.py` as the only
measurement. All thirteen functions landed on 2026-10-30/31 and 2026-11-01 went
that way, several of them after a permuter run
of six figures had failed to improve on the park's own base, and one of them
(func_800345B8, 546 words) the largest function landed in the project. func_80059F8C is the caution on hypotheses
formed from a park's findings rather than from the dumps: its 101 DIFF read
like fenced memory and every `volatile` spelling made it worse, the cause
being loop.c's block placement. func_8004E9EC is the caution on asm devices:
three of them had bought 33 words / 5 DIFF and could not buy the last
register swap, which one 3-byte struct copy -- a block move whose scratch
clobbers prune the parameter's preference -- gave for free. When a park's
remainder is a register assignment no source shape seems to reach, ask what
RTL IDIOM the target's instruction order implies before adding a device.
func_800345B8 is the caution on a park's own diagnosis: it carried three
confident mechanism claims across two passes and all three were wrong, while
the thing that landed it -- where two pointer assignments sit -- was never
named in any of them. Re-derive a park's diagnosis from the dumps; do not
inherit it. func_8004B954 is the caution on reading a park's DIFF: its 247
looked hopeless and was two words of LENGTH plus a positional shift, with
three register-only rows underneath. **Read `advise.py`'s aligned verdict,
never the positional DIFF, when deciding what a park is worth.** A triage in
`docs/carved-sweep.md` that called it a wrong-shape body was wrong.
func_8005B4D0 is the caution on a lever the park itself recorded: its two scan
loops had been written as explicit `goto`s on a 2026-10-27 lever worth 10
words, and that is exactly what held the function -- a goto loop carries no
`LOOP_BEG` note, loop.c never sees it, and nothing gets hoisted. **A lever is
evidence about the function it was measured on.** Reading the `.loop` dump,
which had never been read for that park, turned 333 DIFF into 76 and priced
the hoist decision in numbers the dump prints. func_8004CECC is the caution on
a park's own list of what a thing would take: its row named three ways the
target's copy could have survived jump2 and said no natural spelling produced
one, and the third way -- a non-SI mode -- was a one-byte struct away. **A
"nothing produces this" note is a record of what was tried, not a proof.**
And func_8005B4D0's second pass is the caution on a constant read out of a
dump: it corrected the first pass's `n_non_fixed_regs` from 28 to 30 on the
strength of a printed product, but the 62 it factored came from loop.c:3241's
strength-reduction threshold, `(loop_has_call ? 1 : 2) * (3 +
n_non_fixed_regs)`, not from move_movables' `1 + n_non_fixed_regs` at :532 --
two formulas three apart. 28 was right, and the giv line is a second witness
for it. **When a dump prints a product, check which formula produced it
before factoring.** Do not read
`docs/landing-a-function.md` as requiring the search
tools on these; its contracts (the landing gate, the DEVICE and park
contracts) still hold.

`func_8003CAA8` is not a function: it is `main`'s unreachable epilogue, split
off because it follows `main`'s infinite loop. Landing it is a spec change --
extend the `main` asm subsegment to 0x102c4 and start the C unit there.

**The first thing to know is that the 512 KB file is not the program.** The

Of the 105,668 `data` + `rodata` bytes, **31,752 are PSY-Q library data and
rodata** placed at module granularity, and 73,916 are the game's. Two things
made that possible, both on 2026-11-01 and both written up in
`docs/data-map.md`:

- **The library stamp.** Every PSY-Q library carries one 8-byte record
  `50 73 <id> 15 9C <ck> 40 00` -- ASCII "Ps" plus a library id -- at offset 0
  of one designated module's section. All 18 libraries in `psyq/lib` carry
  one, so the id is a lookup table, and the seven in this executable each mark
  a section start exactly.
- **`.data` scanning.** `azure libscan` matches modules with relocation
  masking but only over `.text`. The same method applied to `.data` and
  `.rdata` places 31 library module sections with zero mismatching words,
  including all four libgte tables (`rsin_tbl`, `SQRT`, `rcossin_tbl`,
  `ratan_tbl`) and libc's `_ctype_`. 42 real PSY-Q names came out of the
  modules' export tables.

libraries' `.rdata`, not `.data` -- splat had been saying so on every run --
and the libcd/libgpu boundary was 24 bytes too late, filing libgpu/PRIM's
`"tpage: (%d,%d,%d,%d)"` under libcd. And the claim that
`ld_legacy_generation` groups a segment's subsegments by section, which two
yaml comments and `data-map.md` all asserted, is **false**; the real

0x3E01C is the SN module descriptor, and it self-validates: `__text` +
`__textlen` is exactly `__data`, and `__data` + `__datalen` is exactly `_gp`.

## Before writing a function

Read `docs/matching-levers.md` -- the lever index for this compiler. Add to it
whenever a session establishes a new one.

Check the compilation context before sweeping source shapes; a bound computed
in the wrong context is void, not weakened. The context is: which functions
share the translation unit and in what order, each symbol's declared type and
size, the `-G` value, the struct layout, the flags.

## Hazards that have each cost a day

- **`make clean` deletes `asm/` and `assets/`, not just `build/`.** Recovery is
  `make clean && make splat && make`. Do not wire `splat` in as a build
  prerequisite -- it re-splits the 26 MB dungeon container.
- **`make splat` only clears `asm/nonmatchings`.** `SFILES` is a `find` over
  `asm/`, so a removed or renamed subsegment leaves its old `.s` behind and it
  keeps getting linked. Link failures on symbols from a deleted segment mean
  orphans in `asm/`.
- **Do not run `make splat` while anything reads those listings.**
- **FIXED 2026-11-02 by going native: concurrent `ccpsx` runs under one wine
  prefix cross-contaminate.** Measured 2026-10-29: at 8-way parallelism with
  distinct inputs, 2 of 80 outputs were *another input's* object, complete with
  its `.file` symbol -- a wrong answer that assembles. A per-invocation `TEMP`
  does not fix it (5/80 to 2/80); one prefix per parallel slot does (0/80).
  Nothing runs the compiler under wine any more (`tools/cdkgcc`), so this
  cannot happen; `tools/ccpsx` and the pool were deleted on 2026-11-02.
- **Every tool that shells out to the compiler must pass `-Ipsyq/include`**,
  not just `-Iinclude`. The failure is a misleading
  `gnu/stubs-32.h: No such file or directory` from the host headers, on every
  file at once, with `make` still green.
- **`verify.py` compares only the upper 16 bits of any relocated word.** A
  `%lo(SYM)+N` where the target has `%lo(SYM)` passes, and so does a jump-table
  reference pointing at the wrong place. `make check` is the only authority;
  treat a verify MATCH as "worth running make check on".
- **Declaration decides addressing.** A scalar or an array within `-G8` gets
  the assembler-expanded symbolic form; anything larger or an incomplete
  `extern u16 X[];` gets the compiler split.
- **splat's `%hi`/`%lo` rendering hides the difference** between a symbol
  reference and a constant address: a bare `lui reg, 0x8001` prints as
  `%hi(SYM)` whenever splat can pair it with a later `%lo`. Globals outside the
  loaded image (0x8002d000..0x8008875f) were constant addresses in the
  original, reached through a constant base pointer with the offset inside the
  index expression -- see func_80043568 in `csrc/unk_14934.c`. A symbol array
  with a *variable* index always materialises the whole address, so declaring
  such a symbol is a dead end.
- **The permuter's scorer handles relocations on both sides**, so a constant
  address raises the floor rather than breaking the search -- the penalty is
  constant and does not change the ranking. Only `func_8004AC3C` among the
  parks uses the idiom. `objdump_command` in a function's `settings.toml`
  resolves it if the zero floor is wanted.
- **Both of these died with wine on 2026-11-02** and are kept only so a
  stale listing or an old scratch file is recognisable: `cc1psx` output had
  CRLF line endings, so the CR had to be stripped before matching labels; and
  wine's filesystem was case-insensitive, so `zz_gA.s` and `zz_ga.s` were one
  file, the compiler wrote the wrong one, exited 0, and the caller died with
  FileNotFoundError on a file it had "written". The native compiler emits LF
  and writes the path given to `-o`.
- **grep needs `-a` on the psyq headers** (CRLF plus extended ASCII makes grep
  treat them as binary and find nothing silently).
- **Scratch belongs in `scratch/`.** `.gitignore` covers `scratch/`, `zz*.c`
  and `b[0-9]*.py`.
- **A park's rejected variant is evidence only against the source it was
  measured on.** After any shape in a function changes, re-measure the
  rejections. `search.py` does this mechanically and costs no tokens.
- **`search.py` has been run greedily over every park within 8 DIFF** (17 of
  them, 2026-10-27) and no single change improved on any base. It explores
  source shapes, so it cannot reach a `local-alloc`/`sched1` tie-break. It is
  still the right first move on a park whose remainder is not yet diagnosed.

## Running agents on this

A fresh `general-purpose` agent pointed at `docs/matching-levers.md` costs far
less than a fork of the main session: measured 212K tokens / 47 tool calls for
func_80044D24 against ~890K / 326 for a fork, which inherits the whole
conversation and pays for it every turn. Give it the function, the documents to
read, and an explicit instruction not to run `make splat`, not to touch
`csrc/`, and not to commit.

Do not kill a long-running agent -- its measurements are in its context. Ask it
to report what it has, which caps its remaining turns and still yields them.

**Measured again on 2026-11-02, over twelve agents in two batches of six, all
twelve of which landed.** Cost per agent ran 77K-127K tokens (median ~100K),
17-44 tool calls, 2.5-12 minutes wall clock, for functions of 25-82 words. The
spread is almost entirely the LEAD: the cheapest runs were the ones handed a
landed relative with its line number and a hazard screen already done, the
dearest were the ones that had to settle an arity from a grandcaller or search
a 28-point spelling grid. Six in parallel is the right width -- they do not
contend, and the main session can land small functions by hand meanwhile, which
is how 29 functions came out of two agent batches.

Two things that only show up at that width. **Agents working in parallel
produce signature CONFLICTS that each of them measures as 0 differing**, because
a narrow parameter or a pointer type can be invisible inside one function and
decided only by the other -- two such conflicts in one batch, both resolved at
merge time by re-measuring in the unit. And **a landed function's agent REPORT
is worth more than its code**, because it carries the call-site readings: three
functions landed from recipes handed over in another agent's report without
their dumps being read at all. Ask every agent, explicitly, what it established
about its callees and callers.

`docs/town-sweep.md` carries the brief and a measured list of what in it is
load-bearing. The short version: a landed relative quoted with its line number,
the word count read off the listing header, the jump-table and rodata screen
done for them, a unique scratch filename, verification in a merged copy of the
unit with a positive-control count, and a demand for a table of every spelling
measured rather than a verdict.
